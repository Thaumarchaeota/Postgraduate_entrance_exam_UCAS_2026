| 时间承载词（助动词）👉<br>态（动词） -  不变 👇 | 现在时 do    | 过去时 did            | 将来时 will do    | 过去将来 would do   |
| ------------------------------ | --------- | ------------------ | -------------- | --------------- |
| 一般态 be do                      | do        | did                | will do        | would do        |
| 进行态 be doing                   | doing     | was/were doing     | will doing     | would doing     |
| 完成态 have done                  | have done | had done           | will have done | would have done |
| 完成进行 been done                 | been done | was/were been done | will been done | would been done |
- 一般态 (进行态)
	- 现在时：
		- 一般现在：一般性/规律性
		- 现在进行：强调正在
	- 过去时：
		- 过去进行：强调当时持续
	- 将来时
		- 事先安排好的事实一般用现在进行/一般现在
- 完成态：关注点在于什么时候完成了，看截止时间

#### 小作文
###### 历年真题
- 10: You are supposed to write for the Postgraduates’ Association a notice to recruit volunteers for an international conference on globalization. The notice should include the basic qualifications for applicants and the other information which you think is relevant. 
	- Key: **通知、招募会议志愿者**
- 11: Write a letter to a friend of yours to: (1) recommend one of your favorite movies and (2) give reasons for your recommendation.
	- Key: **信件熟人、推荐电影**
- 12: Some international students are coming to your university. Write them an email in the name of the Students’ Union to (1) extend your welcome and (2) provide some suggestions for their campus life here.
	- Key: **信件生人、欢迎国际学生**
- 13: Write an e-mail of about 100 words to a foreign teacher in your college, inviting him/her to be a judge for the upcoming English speech contest.You should include the details you think necessary.
	- Key: **信件生人、邀请外教当裁判**
- 14: Write a letter of about 100 words to the president of your university, suggesting how to improve students’ physical condition.
	- Key: **信件生人、建议提升环境**
- 15: You are going to host a club reading session. Write an email of about 100 words recommending a book to the club members.
	- Key: **信件生人、推荐书**
- 16: Suppose you are a librarian in your university.Write a notice of about 100 words, providing the newly-enrolled international students with relevant information about the library.
	- Key: **通知、给新生介绍图书馆**
- 17: You are to write an email to James Cook , a newly-arrived Australian professor, recommending some tourist attractions in your city. Please give reasons for your recommendation.
	- Key: **信件生人、介绍旅游景点**
- 18: Write an email to all international experts on campus inviting them to attend the graduation ceremony. In your email you should include time, place and other relevant information about the ceremony.
	- Key: **信件生人、邀请专家参加毕业典礼**
- 19: Suppose you are working for the “Aiding Rural Primary School” project of your university, write a letter to answer the inquiry from an international student volunteer.
	- Key: **回信生人、招募志愿者**
- 20: The Student Union of your university has assigned you to inform the international students an upcoming singing contest. Write a notice in about 100 words.
	- Key: **通知、歌唱比赛**
- 21: One foreign friend of yours has recently graduated from college and intends to find a job in China. Please write an email to him/her to make some suggestions.
	- Key: **信件熟人、找工作建议**
- 22: Write an email to a professor at a British university, inviting him/her to organize a team for the international innovation contest to be held at your university.
	- Key: **信件生人、介绍**
- 23: Write a notice to recruit a student for Prof. Smith’s research project on campus sports activities. Specify the duties and requirements of the job.
	- Key: **通知、招募受试者**

######
- 第一句：寒暄/问好/表明身份
	- 尊敬：
		- I hope this email finds you well; my name is Li Ming, and I am a student at this university.
		- I hope this email finds you well; my name is \[Your Name], and I am currently completing my final year as an undergraduate majoring in \[Major] at \[University].
	- 熟人
		- Hi \[Name], hope you're **doing well** and everything's been great with you.
	- 半生不熟
		- Hi \[Name], it's \[Your Name] from the \[Club Name]; hope you're doing well.
		- we're in the same \[course/class] this **semester**
	- 通知
		- A \[research program] on \[sports activities], \[led] by Professor Smith’s Lab, is currently underway.
- 第二句：写明目的
	- 真诚
		- 邀请：We **would be most grateful** if you could **lend your expertise** as a \[judge] at the forthcoming \[xxx contest/conference], **time permitting**.
		- 建议：I wish to offer (constructive/practical) (suggestions/measures/feedback) regarding the current arrangement of the \[program] and propose possible improvements.
		- 欢迎：We are delighted to welcome you to \[University/Program], and we look forward to supporting your academic and personal growth.
	- 中性
		- 推荐：I (wanted to share/am writing to **pass along**) a brief recommend for a \[name]
			- 有名：which has long been celebrated throughout \[region] for its \[features]、which has recently gained widespread popularity for its \[features]
				- 景点：breathtaking natural scenery、rich cultural and historical heritage
				- 书籍/电影：lyrical, elegant prose、profound thematic depth、meticulous scholarship
		- 介绍
			- **Please allow me to bring to your attention** the \[place/project]
				- which offers \[extensive print and digital collections and specialized research support].
				- which organizes \[volunteer tutoring, literacy workshops and cultural events for local schools].
		- 招募
			- We are issuing this announcement to solicit applications for \[a research assistant] to help advance the \[program].
- 第二段：主要内容
	- 活动举办
		- The \[contest], **aimed at** \[improving students' spoken‑English skills], will be held on September 27 in the \[Main Auditorium]. 
	- 招募通知
		- The program **focuses on** \[domestic college students' participation in sports] and will employ a suite of statistical methods.
		- **As such**, candidates **are expected to have** a \[strong statistical] background; **Proficiency with** \[statistical software (e.g. SPSS, Stata, R)] **would be preferable**.
		- Responsibilities **encompass** \[task1], \[task2] and occasional participation in \[activity].
- 结尾
	- 招聘
		- Interested candidates should send an application email with a CV and **brief cover letter** to lihua@edu.cn **by** November 27.
		- We would welcome your enthusiastic participation
	- 邀请
		- We would be honored by your participation; please inform us of your availability **at your earliest convenience**.
